The Imperial is a 6,000 square foot stylish, state-of-the-art private event and multimedia facility located in the city’s urban core. Carefully transformed from its abandoned Asian movie house state to its contemporary revitalized form, it features state of the art audio and visual equipment. It is Vancouver’s hidden gem.

Imperial has stepped up in a big way with their awesome venue and some of the best audio visual guys in the area. Its a premier space, a premier team, and they are excited about working with us to bring a great experience.

We know it came down to the wire on the venue this year, but we think we came out for the better. Please make sure to thank a staff member if you see them, and consider them for future events.

Things to note:

- The venue is available to minors provided they are accompanied by their parents. (Assuming the bars are not open)
 - Our capacity is 375 people. We'd like to be at capacity.
