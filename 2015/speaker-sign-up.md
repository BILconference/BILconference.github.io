We are so excited about the overwhelming response and support for BIL 2015. We encourage you send us your proposed talk by filling out the speaker application form.

Confirmation of Stage 1 will be sent to chosen presenters on Thursday at 12:00 pm.

All other presenters will be send a message informing them that they can sign up their talks for their preferred time in Speaking Areas 2 or 3 when they register in the morning on Saturday and Sunday.

If you'd prefer, and don't care about having your talk and bio on the website you may just show up the morning of the day you want to speak and put yourself on the whiteboards in the time slot that you want!

If you have not done so already please make sure you read the rules about speaking at BIL. There are only a few, but they are important! [RULES.](http://2015.bilconference.com/participate/speak/)
