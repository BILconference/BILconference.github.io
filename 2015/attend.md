Important things to know about attending:
- Everyone participates in some way. You may not be a full fledged volunteer, but we has you help up make sure everything is running smoothly, and help out your fellow attendees.
- Have a great time.
- Your donations are what keep this running. Please consider making the suggested donation at the door. We don't just make up this number out of thin air. Its about the amount we need based on estimated attendance for us to cover this years costs and to help pay for the venue for the following year.
- Encourage your friends to attend with you even if you can only make it for part of the event.
- Treat others with respect. Everyone is part of the BIL family here. You're a BILder.
- RSVP/Register early both here and on our Facebook Event!
- If you can't attend, but would like to contribute to the event, please see our [Donation page](http://2015.bilconference.com/contribute/donate)
