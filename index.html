The unconference for people changing the world in big ways

Minds, not badges. Talks, not tickets. 2008–2018 — the complete archive.

In 2008, a group of friends got a crazy idea: crash TED. They wanted the conversations without the price tag — so in two weeks they booked a small venue down the street in Monterey, invited speakers, landed on NPR and in the LA Times, and threw a free, open, participant-run counter-conference. In an Excellent Adventure sort of way, they called it BIL.

For the next decade BIL happened wherever someone decided it should — an old Masonic Temple in Long Beach, the Queen Mary, a converted movie house in Vancouver, college campuses in Delhi and Salem, rooftops in Tunis, a bar in Davos during the World Economic Forum. Everyone stood on equal footing: the attendees were the speakers, the speakers were the attendees, and anyone with fifteen minutes and an idea got a stage. TED eventually answered the grassroots energy with TEDx; BIL just kept being BIL.

This site is the archive of all of it — every event, speaker, and talk that survived, restored from the original bilconference.com and preserved as it was written.
