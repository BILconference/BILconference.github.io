Everyone comes from a long line of dead people. Those dramas and traumas live on in us. They left us their DNA and a Blueprint for survival. Most often we get trapped in their Family Home Movie and cannot seem to escape the same old, same old patterns both negative and positive that we acquired by being born. There is a way out. I can inspire you and show you how to expand your consciousness and horizons beyond your current reality into a grander Picture of your life that you can be the Star in and Direct too!
