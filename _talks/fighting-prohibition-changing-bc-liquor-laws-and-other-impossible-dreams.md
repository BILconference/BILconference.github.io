Corinne Lea recounts how BC's liquor board granted her Rio Theatre a liquor license in 2012 on the condition it stop showing movies, threatening the venue's survival. She describes the four-month public campaign — with city council and community support — that overturned a 1920s-era law separating alcohol and cinemas, and argues arts-and-culture expertise should inform liquor regulation.
