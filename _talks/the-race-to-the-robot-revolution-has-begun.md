Eric Shuss surveys the arrival of robots and artificial intelligence — humanoid androids, expressive robot heads, and AI drug discovery — and argues robots should take physically, emotionally, and dangerously hard jobs while creating new industries, as PCs did. He proposes making Southern California the "Robo Coast," citing its ports, universities, and movie-industry animatronics.
