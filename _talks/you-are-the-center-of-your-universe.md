As we create content online we develop our online identity, influence and expertise. Every Flickr photo, every blog post, every link on delicious, every tweet is a small piece of a complete picture that other people are collecting to determine our influence and expertise. Therefore, it’s imperative that there is some central location where potential clients, friends, colleagues can discover one’s social content in order to truly understand one’s influence/expertise. This is the act of building a personal brand. How can we use various plugins, techniques, etc to expose our influence and expertise?
