Climate change may be the greatest challenge now facing humanity and requires all hands on deck. Accordingly, Josh will break from his BIL tradition of speaking on consciousness or biohacking to illustrate how technology exists today that could easily double public-transit ridership and make a serious dent in civilization's carbon footprint.
