some of simple things are most difficult once you follow. Mr Ilango shares his recipe of happiness

positive thinking : convert adversity to advantage if you have positive thinking. Seen things are beautiful. But Unseen things are more beautiful.
Mr ilango has no regrets because he lost sight. he enjoys it because some of the best moments or most initmate movements of our life can be see
when you close you eyes. you see your self deeper. Mr Ilango is fortunately visually challenged since birth.

Involvement in fine arts : spent quite a lot of time in music. it motivate’s him to be more perfect.

encouraging friends : he has lovely friend who are responsible for his success

self confidence : was teaching in madras university. resigned his job to set up his own company. a brave decision because he wanted to inspire others. Today he meet over 10K youth per year and gives autographs to them. Mr Ilango gets motivated on meeting youths

Open mindness/willingness to learn : keep the third eye open. listening fetches greater benefits.

Humor : humor plays a vital role in our life. it helps sometimes in embarrassing situations.

quest for challenges – always wait for challenges. result is presence of mind

Challenges are always there, but Mr Ilango has faced them bravely.
Once Mr Jack asked Mr Ilango u do not see the world, u see the face then why do not you think of committing suicide. Mr Ilango said that “i thought of that several time, but now after seeing u i have given up”. If Mr Jack who is so insensitive about his emotions then Mr Ilango has all rights to leave.

All the audience and Mr Ilango repeat “be happy and make others happy”. Finally he ends his speech with a song dedicated to all the lovely audience
