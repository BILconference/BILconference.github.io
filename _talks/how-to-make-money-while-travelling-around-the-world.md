Ricky Shetty of DaddyBlogger.com explains how travel blogging funds his family's trips, describing pitches to tourism boards that yield comped hotels, attractions, and meals in exchange for blog reviews and social media coverage. He outlines building a blog — branding, niche audience, posting frequency, marketing — and lists monetization methods including affiliate marketing, sponsored posts, courses, and coaching.
