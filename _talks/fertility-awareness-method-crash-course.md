The Fertility Awareness Method crash course is designed to empower women to know more about their bodies and natural rhythms. Using simple biofeedback tools such as daily body temperature, women can track their cycles. Having this information enables women to know when they ovulate, start a new cycle, chart patterns, and have an overall understanding of one's body. Danielle has been using FAM for 3 years and has taught adolescent girls and moms. She looks forward to educating the clever ladies of BIL (and any men who may be interested)!
