Josh Whiton links consciousness to complexity and argues that raising it — through trauma resolution, breath work, music, and experiences like the astronaut overview effect — improves governance, markets, and behavior generally. He contends many consciousness-elevating practices exist among therapists and teachers but lack scale and investment, and proposes a venture fund directing capital toward elevating human consciousness.
