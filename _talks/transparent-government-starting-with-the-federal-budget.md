What is the role we can all play in regards to making Transparent Government a reality? Our first use case is a big one... The transparent federal budget (yah the US one) I dare say one on the largest budgets on the planet. If we can succeed here then the rest of the governance issues are easy! I have a plan. I will first outline it and then we will all talk about the "sticky" bits.
