Allison explores the politics, privileges and identity issues endemic to creating art. In this talk, Allison will deconstruct notions of privilege, class, friv and guilt into useful components for creativity, discuss the intersection between art and social justice, and share some methods for distilling passion from conflict to turn activism into relevant art.
