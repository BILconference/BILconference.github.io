Colin Mangham introduces biomimicry through shark-inspired innovations, from the banned Speedo LZR racing suit to Sharklet's dermal-denticle surfaces that inhibit bacterial growth in hospitals. He surveys other examples including Velcro, whale-inspired turbine blades, and dolphin-based undersea modems, and argues that curiosity and affection drive conservation of species like sharks, of which some 100 million are killed yearly.
