Have you ever caught yourself living the same day over and over again? The change of faces and places does little to shift our conditioned responses and ideas. We expect ourselves to act and think a certain way and so we do! We discover our limits and spend the rest of our lives playing within them. It seems that we are bound to be "ourselves". Or are we? In this experiential talk Boris introduces a fresh alternative to living within the boundaries of the social matrix by tapping into the unlimited field of spontaneity - an exploration that puts you quite literally at the leading edge of life.
