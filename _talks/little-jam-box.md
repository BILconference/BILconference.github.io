Kathy "Kmom" Huffman introduces the Musicians, Artists and Poets Society, a nonprofit founded by retired auditing professor Rick Hayes, and its "little jam box" — a little-free-library-style container for giving away CDs, art, and poetry. Hayes describes wanting exposure for unheard local musicians and the society's no-strings website platform for artists.
