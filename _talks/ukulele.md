Amanda Palmer performed "Ukulele" (likely "Ukulele Anthem") and, with Jason Webley, took audience questions. Recapping the event on her blog, Amanda Palmer wrote: "jason and i talked and did a q&a with the peoples of vancouver about artistry and money and street performance and how musicians and artists feel weird about money."
