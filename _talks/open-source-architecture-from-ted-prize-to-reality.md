It has been two years since winning the TED Prize and the birth of an idea and website called the Open Architecture Network - the worlds first open source (access) site for architectural solutions to humanitarian crises. This will be a critical look at whether it is working. Also a brief look ahead and at the 2009 Open Architecture Challenge: Classroom which launches the week before at Davos.
