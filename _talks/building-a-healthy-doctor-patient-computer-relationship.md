Your medical relationship has a third-wheel, and it's not human. It's a database, and it has the power to have your records and to hold them from this day forward, for better or worse, in sickness and health (even until after death do you part).

While the right data with the right person at the right time improves patient health while lowering overall costs, the use of technology by patients, doctors, and payers alike is hurting the experiences and perceptions of people within the healthcare ecosystem. Learn from a healthcare technology expert why your health and happiness should be in the hands of doctors instead of computers. It's time to take our medical relationships back.
