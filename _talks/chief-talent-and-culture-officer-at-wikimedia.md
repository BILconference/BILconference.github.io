Gayle Karen Young describes her role at the Wikimedia Foundation and her work on internet freedom and human rights, citing government surveillance, censorship, and arrests over social media posts, plus projects like the Oslo Freedom Forum and Disrupt North Korea. She then turns personal, discussing depression, divorce, and loss as sources of capacity, and frames choices around liberating structures, competence, uncertainty, and joy.
