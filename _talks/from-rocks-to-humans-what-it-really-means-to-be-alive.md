The Big Bang theory explains how atoms came into existence and the theory of evolution explains how a simple cell can evolve into complex human beings. But how do you go from the stuff that makes up rocks to the stuff that makes up living things? I will explain the mechanisms, forces, and intermediary steps that connect atoms to rocks and rocks to humans in order to fill in the blanks of what it really means to be alive.
