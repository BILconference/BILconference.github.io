Each of our voices is an incredible asset that needs to be captured, owned, and valued. We each need to take leadership, share our ideas and connect with others to build the needed structures of change. How do we do this? How do we come together in the spirit of cooperation to identify the critical path of inquiry that can bring us together within a collective narrative? We will look at the power of the voice, our fundamental freedoms, the array of technologies that can help to facilitate communication and collaboration, and the real world actions that are required to integrate this work.
