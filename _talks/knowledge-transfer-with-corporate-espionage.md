Until we have The Young Ladies Illustrated Primer to teach street smarts for The Diamond Age, we need tools to facilitate knowledge transfer.

Join me for an overview of the new card game Corporate Espionage. We'll go briefly over the business mentorship program that accompanies it and learn to play the game. Corporate Espionage is a great team building activity for businesses and schools to participate in.
