Anil Pattni tells how losing a job and a relationship led him to found OC Hackers, a community-led technology incubator in Orange County. He describes adopting the hackathon model as an educational framework, and events including the OC Mini Maker Faire, a kids' PC-reassembly competition, a hardware hackathon, and the Startup Bus to South by Southwest.
