Brent Bushnell traces Two Bit Circus's path through immersive entertainment experiments — a spy-themed adventure, location-based GPS games predating Pokémon Go, VR brand activations, and a STEAM carnival with a foundation teaching kids to build their own games. He previews the company's forthcoming "micro amusement park" in downtown Los Angeles and discusses lessons about prototyping and finding conviction in ideas.
