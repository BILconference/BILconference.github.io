Alex Wormuth presents HexCare, a platform aggregating data from wearables and smart health devices — blood pressure, glucose, weight, sleep, activity — so patients can share it with doctors. Using his diabetic, hypertensive grandmother as an example, he argues that infrequent office readings miss dangerous blood pressure spikes, and describes pilots with a Pasadena physician and a children's hospital.
