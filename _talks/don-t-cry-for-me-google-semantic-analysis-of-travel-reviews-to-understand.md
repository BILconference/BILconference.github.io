Conventional search engines deliver documents which include significant occurrences of keywords in user queries. … To enable such feeling-based recommendation, we built a knowledge base and reasoning engine which operates with entities of the travel domain. We also constructed a formal model of human sentiments and feelings to be extracted from text and to serve as a basis for providing recommendations.
