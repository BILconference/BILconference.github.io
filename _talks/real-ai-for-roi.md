Real AI for ROI: Can commercial AGI power us to Escape Velocity?

AGI-based virtual agents offer ROI at three levels:

1. To the company employing them (ROI: infinite!)
2. To the suppliers’ investors (ROI: pretty damn good)
3. To futurists (ROI: escape velocity?)

I’ll present a plan, concrete numbers, and a possible timeline to achieve these objectives.
