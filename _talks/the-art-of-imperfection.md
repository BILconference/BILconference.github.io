A thought provoking look at yourself from within. Images of perfection are everywhere: TV, the internet, magazines, not to mention our fantasies and dreams of being someone we are not. This is a reminder to all of us that we don’t need to be perfect to be successful and happy. We will look at the art of being disorganized, silly, wrong and neither rich or famous.
