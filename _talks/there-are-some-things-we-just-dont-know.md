How many angels can dance on the head of a pin? When does life begin? What do we know? Are there some things we never will have answers for? In our race for science, faith and reason, the hubris of man is the detrimental obstacle to greater understanding and wisdom. Gordy Grundy will read his piece that was first performed at 'Inauguration Subversion' at Stories in Echo Park. He will then open the topic for discussion.
