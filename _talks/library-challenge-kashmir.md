She has an inspiring story to tell. It’s about the resilience and resourcefulness of even the poorest people recovering from a massive natural disaster (earthquake of 2005) and how they chose to do so in an most unusual way: by holding Kashmir’s first ever book fairs and starting their own community libraries!
