A panel with Joseph Vaughn-Perling, Jared Kenna, Rebecca Ahn, and a BitPay representative discusses Bitcoin's benefits: controlling one's own wealth, cheap remittances, and banking for the unbanked and high-inflation countries. They predict a future of multiple cryptocurrencies, decentralized autonomous corporations, and micropayments, and field audience questions on exchange failures like Mt. Gox, altcoins, and SEC/CFTC regulation.
