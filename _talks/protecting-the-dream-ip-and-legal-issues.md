Intellectual property attorney Burke advises creators on legal protection before launching: trademark searches and intent-to-use applications, why ideas and facts are not copyrightable, and the Shepard Fairey Obama-poster case. She covers fashion design protection through patterns and deliberate flaws, design patents, licensing risks, and unresolved SEC rules around crowdfunding rewards.
