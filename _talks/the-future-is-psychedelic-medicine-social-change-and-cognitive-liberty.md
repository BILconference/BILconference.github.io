Ashley Booth argues for renewed psychedelic research, reviewing LSD and psilocybin relief of cluster headaches, MAPS's MDMA-assisted psychotherapy for PTSD, and ibogaine treatment for opiate addiction. She discusses why off-patent, single-use drugs attract no pharmaceutical development, the Controlled Substances Act's research barriers, and her Aware Project's events and monthly salons.
