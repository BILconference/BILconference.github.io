Any new ideas to cut the gordian knots in the Middle East? I have one that I'll present & would love to hear others. I'll also present what I see as relevant background to the situation and, again, would love to hear what others see as relevant background. Our perspective on the Middle East is influenced by our own heritage, how far back in time we look, our notions of fairness, and our understanding of "realpolitik".
