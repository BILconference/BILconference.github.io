Metabolic syndrome and Alzheimers, two leading causes of death in our modern world, and the underlying mechanisms that link the diseases. We’ll delve deep into the claim that Alzheimer's is like "Type 3 Diabetes". We’ll explore example data that dementia researchers work with in diagnosing neuropsychological problems, using software developed by Clinton to help visualize the results.
