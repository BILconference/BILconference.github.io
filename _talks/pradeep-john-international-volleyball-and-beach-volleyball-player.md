Pradeep John, talking about his life experience, in a different ball game

As a child he was very active and mischief kid, being very tall was advantages and disadvantages

School teacher took him to vollyball

Started vollyball, had to leave chennai to go to junior asian champ at quatar, camp was in Bangalore, was a different experience

Had to stay with 24 national level athelets

Learned team spirit, dicipline, hardwork passion to achieve something

Din’t get selct for the first 12 team

didn’t give up , was about to board the flight, but news came that tournament was cancelled

Later won the junior world champ
Injury time, dreams shattered, one month rest, couldn’t even walk. didn’t leave the passion

Came back after the surgery, was really difficult to get back to national

shifted to beach vollyball, 2 member game

Became India’s no:1 beach volly ball player

Players have to fight against talents and vested inetersts
