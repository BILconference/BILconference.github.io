Story Money Impact is an initiative that seeks to increase connections between those passionate about the power of art for social change. Bringing together storytellers, funders and activists is not only beneficial for each of them, but for society as a whole. With frontline tales from the NFB, Good Pitch and Roundhouse Radio, this talk suggests that given the right partnerships local media can impact hearts, minds, behaviours - and public policy.
