Finding your purpose is actually inspired by the feeling of unhappiness, discomfort, and sometimes what appears to be misfortune. I'd like to speak with women about the conditioning in love and career that has held us back from finding our purpose in life. I give the examples form my own life of losing my job, relationship and house mate to opening a world of love and inspiration by starting a nonprofit, SYRV and AFFIRMITS.
