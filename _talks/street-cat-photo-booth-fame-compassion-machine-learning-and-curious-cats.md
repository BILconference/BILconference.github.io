Jordan Matthew Yerman describes building Street Cat Photo Booth with computer scientist Lee Smith: a Raspberry Pi camera inside a plastic tub that lets feral cats trigger their own photos, posted to Instagram with proceeds benefiting cat rescue groups. He recounts field tests in Brooklyn alleys and a Vancouver junkyard, and plans for machine-learning image selection.
