Challenging the boundaries of Conferences. How I, and others, have gained access to TED and other conferences. Photos of TEDs past. Techniques and ways in, and how to avoid being kicked out. Definitions of acceptable behavior. How gleaning information and PR materials can enrich your, network, knowledge and social base. Why can't more conferences be unconferences. What can we do to promote and further the practice of Conference Crashing. This is meant to serve as a summary of topics for my talk followed by discussion. As I am not sure of my talks time alotment I will be working on several different scripts, Short Medium and Long. I had an extremely good time at the first BiL and mentioned the idea of presenting. So let me know what you think. Chris From Monterey.
