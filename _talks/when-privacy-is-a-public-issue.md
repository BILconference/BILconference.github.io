Reichart Von Wolfsheild recounts a four-year legal battle stemming from the Scott Rothstein Ponzi scheme: after Rothstein invested in his company, bankruptcy lawyers subpoenaed all data on his task-management system, including other clients' private information. He describes refusing on Fourth Amendment principle, being held in contempt, and the fee incentives he argues drive bankruptcy litigation.
