Judd Weiss explains his self-taught photography approach, covering the exposure triangle of shutter speed, ISO, and aperture and their trade-offs, his preference for wide apertures, black-and-white JPEGs, and mirrorless cameras. He discusses composition as glorifying rather than documenting subjects, filling the frame, and his assertive style of directing portrait subjects.
