Evita is going to share her journey of conscious motherhood - inspired by visions, supported by holistic life-style, raw vegan pregnancy, the birth story of her son Jonathon, breastfeeding and attachment parenting. She is launching a campaign for conscious motherhood, for the sake of well-being of moms and babies, for the sake of world peace.
