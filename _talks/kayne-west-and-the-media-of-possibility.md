Devin O'Neill defends Kanye West's public self-aggrandizement as a deliberate strategy for cultural impact, citing Paul Arden's argument that refusing artificial boundaries enables large-scale creative work. He contends that internet-era controversy grows a cultural conversation while material goals get achieved, compares West to Muhammad Ali, and frames him as a symbol of self-belief and creative possibility.
