Sakthi D is a Chennai-based social activist, motivational speaker, and NLP master trainer who founded The Healer Foundation in April 2016, a non-profit serving needy children, the elderly, and destitute women in Tamil Nadu. He holds degrees in computer science and an MBA, and previously worked as a director and master trainer at IROCK Learning Technologies.
