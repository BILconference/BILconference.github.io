A recent Whammy Award winner, Eddie Lam is a multi-instrumentalist from Vancouver who demonstrates his passion for music through playing on stage and connecting with the audience. His current projects are Rockaoke Live, (a live band karaoke experience) and Cry of Silence, a local all-Asian rock band.
