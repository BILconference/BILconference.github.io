Samuel Eddy is a Chennai-based event curator and networking organizer who founded SAM (Science Art Management) and served as chief coordinator of TEDxChennai. He introduced the BIL Conference format to India, organizing the country's first BIL event, and founded F4, a weekly Chennai networking initiative.
