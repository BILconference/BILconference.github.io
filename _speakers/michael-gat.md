Michael Gat is a career technologist, currently focused on the intersection of data, artificial intelligence and opportunities to make the world better. He was recently project manager for the NSF-run National Transportation Data Challenge and consults to companies ranging from small start-ups to Fortune-100 global brands. He lives in Venice Beach with his cat Flash, who mostly ignores him. Michael is always looking for work.
