Troy Gardner is an interdisciplinarian, who loves bridging tech, art, music, design, delegation, automation, communication. An seasoned performer, now professional introvert who almost never talks to anyone let alone presents, he's happy to help share this method he's discovered and uses sometimes hourly. A long time fan of TED talks, this is his first BIL presentation, and second BIL attendance, despite years of friends nagging him to come.
