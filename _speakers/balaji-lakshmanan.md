Balaji Lakshmanan is a Chennai-based roboticist and entrepreneur who founded Geeky Technology & Consulting (iMakeRobots) in 2008, building Wi-Fi-controlled robots, running robotics workshops for students, and serving as robotics consultant for the Tamil film Mugamoodi. He earlier worked as a project associate at IIT Madras.
