Laura Glass is a feminist porn crusader and the erotic art curator of Montreal's Artists Of Desire. She has collaborated on DIY porn shoots, and given talks on feminist porn in the Adult Industry, as a guest lecturer at McGill University and at PACE Society in Vancouver. Trained in Visual Arts, Design and Web Development, she currently tinkers in Web Development, Fintech and VR.
