Leanna Wolfe is a cultural Anthropologist who began traveling internationally when at 20 she navigated Europe with a youth hostel card, a backpack and a train pass. Since then she has lived in remote villages in the Yucatan, Africa, Borneo and Papua New Guinea and negotiated the complexities of travel in Russia, Malaysia, Indonesia, China, Eastern and Western Europe, South and Central America, Canada, India, the Caribbean and through the U.S.
