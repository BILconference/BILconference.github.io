A. Garrett Lisi is a theoretical physicist working outside academia, known for his 2007 preprint "An Exceptionally Simple Theory of Everything," which proposed unifying all known particles and forces within the geometry of the E8 Lie group and drew wide attention alongside substantial criticism. He presented the theory at the first BIL conference in 2008, the same week as his TED talk, pursuing physics while surfing and snowboarding.
