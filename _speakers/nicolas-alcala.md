Nicolás Alcalá is a Spanish filmmaker who was CEO and co-founder of Future Lighthouse, a VR storytelling studio based in Madrid and Los Angeles. He previously wrote, directed, and produced the crowdfunded film The Cosmonaut, and in 2017 became the first VR director with three projects in competition at the Venice International Film Festival.
