Santhosh Babu is a physician-turned-Indian Administrative Service officer of the 1995 batch, Tamil Nadu cadre, who served as Chairman and Managing Director of the Tamil Nadu Handicrafts Development Corporation (Poompuhar) from 2014, introducing e-commerce, 3D printing, and IT-driven modernization to the state handicrafts body. His earlier postings included District Collector of Sivaganga and Krishnagiri and Managing Director of ELCOT.
