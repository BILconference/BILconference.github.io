An artist whose chosen media is architecture, Jenda Michl runs the architecture company Vertu Studio, with projects that landed on the front page of Dwell.com and exhibited at the Architecture + Design Museum.  Look for his FlexSit bench at BIL this year.  A great proponent of sharing and cooperation, he is also working to create an architectural 'nerve center' to unite and empower the amazing design talent Los Angeles attracts.
