Scott Johnson is the founder and president of the Myelin Repair Foundation (MRF), which established the potential of myelin repair as an alternative to current immunosuppressant therapies for multiple sclerosis and is speeding up the process of bringing those treatments to patients through a new model for conducting medical research.
