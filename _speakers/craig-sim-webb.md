Craig Sim Webb is Executive Director of The non-profit DREAMS Foundation (www.dreams.ca), as well as a professional speaker/trainer/author, dream analyst/researcher, and performing/recording musician. He has participated in pioneering dream and consciousness research at Stanford University and Montreal's Sacre-Coeur Hospital.
