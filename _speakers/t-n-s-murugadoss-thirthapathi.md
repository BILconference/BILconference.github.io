T.N.S. Murugadoss Thirthapathi (1931–2020) was the titular head of the Singampatti Zamin in Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu, crowned its 31st Raja in 1934 at the age of three and often described as India's last crowned ruler. After the zamindari system was abolished in the 1950s he worked for decades as a Life Insurance Corporation agent while continuing ceremonial duties at Singampatti, and authored Musings from a Zamin (2003).
