Isaiah Frizzell is chef and designer of underground supper club pHeast. A rags to dishes fighter in the culinary world and an advocate for the elevation of the dishwasher as perhaps the most important element in a functioning kitchen. He teaches classes on modernist cooking and molecular gastronomy. He plays with liquid nitrogen for people to gawk and has had it poured over him more than once.
