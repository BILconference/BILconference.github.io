"Cathy Browne is a lifestyle, travel, and events photographer who just happens to be legally blind. Her camera captures a world she can’t see.

And she’s good. Take a look:
https://www.flickr.com/photos/cathybrowne/albums

Cathy’s also a veteran PR gal and accessibility advocate – she’s just about to start her third term with the Persons with Disabilities Advisory Committee for the City of Vancouver. Cathy believes in the power of community, and loves connecting people."
