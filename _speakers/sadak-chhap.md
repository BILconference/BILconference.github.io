An aspiring actor, a banker, and a doctor. That is what the three makers of Sadak Chhap – Ankur, Shubham and Ashish – were destined to continue being if YouTube hadn’t happened. Popular faces in the YouTube comedy circle, Ankur and Shubham are relatively new to the milllion-plus subscribers club. However, the channel is only a month old. The comedy duo, along with their off-screen partner Ashish McCune, have been chasing their content creation dream.
