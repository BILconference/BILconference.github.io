Niko Bell is a freelance journalist, writer, photographer and Chinese translator from Vancouver, British Columbia. His work appears extensively in Xtra, Canada's largest LGBT magazine, as well as the CBC, BC Business Magazine, Soar Magazine, the Huffington Post and the Chronicle Herald, among others.
