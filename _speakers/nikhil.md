A musician, composer, and performer, Nikhil (The Tabla Guy) personifies the time-honored tradition of the tabla with a dizzying array of musical styles. Capture the glimpses of his triumph that will go on to linger in your minds forever; the elixir of inspiration- Weaving the hypnotic sounds of the tabla drums into the fabric of various music styles, he spreads it’s rhythmic pulse like a newly found ray of sunshine with a fresh cultural integration. With a fan following of 83k on Instagram and the uniqueness in his art, Nikhil is creating a fan base on Youtube as well.
