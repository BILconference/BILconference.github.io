William Hurley, known as whurley, is an Austin-based technologist and entrepreneur. He co-founded the creative technology studio Chaotic Moon Studios, acquired by Accenture in 2015, and then the fintech startup Honest Dollar, acquired by Goldman Sachs, where he subsequently worked. A former IBM Master Inventor with a background at Apple, he was a frequent speaker at SXSW and other technology conferences.
