Reichart Von Wolfsheild is an American inventor and technologist who served as lead scientist at Prolific Publishing, where he guided development of the Return Fire video game series. He co-hosted the History Channel series Invention USA (2011–2013), developed products for companies including Boeing, Disney, and Mattel, and was a longtime BIL Conference organizer and advisor.
