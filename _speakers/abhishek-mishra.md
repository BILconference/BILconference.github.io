Not just the winner of the title Ironman thrice, Abhishek Mishra is also now one of the Eight Ultraman in India. He has bestowed our country with another feather in its cap. He deserted his job to give way to the ecstasy within him: running. For him, running is a philosophy that leads to spiritual, mental, emotional and physical well being.
