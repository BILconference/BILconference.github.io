**John M. Smart** is CEO of [Foresight Company](http://www.foresightcompany.com) (Los Gatos, CA), a professional foresight speaking, training, and publishing community. He is also president of the [Acceleration Studies Foundation](http://www.accelerating.org) and co-founder of the [Evo](http://www.evodevouniverse.com) Devo Universe international research community. He is adjunct faculty at the University of Houston [Strategic Foresight Masters Program](http://www.houstonforesight.org/), lecturer in technology leadership at the [Naval Postgraduate School](http://www.nps.edu/) (Monterey, CA), and advisor at [Singularity University](http://singularityu.org/) and [NextIT](http://www.nextit.com/). He has an MS in Futures Studies and an MSEq Physiology & Medicine. His book, [*The Foresight Guide*](http://www.foresightguide.com), comes out on Amazon in 2015. Blog: [EverSmarterWorld.com](http://www.eversmarterworld.com).
