Since ’96 Danielle Reyes aka “MiZThANG” dominated golden era local radio airwaves by freestyle rapping on CFRO 102.7fm Co-op Radio’s “Morning Driveby” show hosted by Maximus Clean, & on DJ Flipout & Jay Swing’s “The Worldwide Ride” on 96.1FM. She believes in the present now & hopes to spread her message.
