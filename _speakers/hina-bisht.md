An MBA graduate. A Financial Anaylst. A Leap of Faith. A Food Blogger that touched thousands of hearts with her take on Indian Food. Hina Bisht is one strong headed woman resounding Fun, Food and Frolic. She left her all-time banking job to fill the canvas of her life with colours of food. A look at her unique instagram handle is sure to tingle your taste buds and so are her delectable recipes.
