Lia Oganesyan was a member of the Fall 2016 class of TED Residents and founder of VeeR Hub, an online marketplace for virtual reality content creation. She gave a TED talk titled "Virtual Reality Is Not a Luxury" — the same argument she brought to BIL:Davos — and had earlier co-published DARPA-funded research on virtual human therapists for soldiers with PTSD.
