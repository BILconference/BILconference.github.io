An expert in grief & loss counsel, spirit communication, bodywork & massage, nutrition, and breakthrough coaching, Colleen helps clients to interpret the messages of their body & mind – and those of their loved ones in Spirit – improving how they feel on the inside, so they may attract their lifestyle goals and dreams in faster.

No stranger to loss, Colleen suffered the death of her dad and cousin Robert both to cancer 20 years ago. Dad and Rob passed within 9 months of each other, followed by the loss of several loved ones annually for the next 8 years.

Unsure if the death experience was an end or renewal of life, Colleen’s experience of her loved ones desire to communicate through Spirit Signs paved the way to believing a soul’s life continues and contributed to her passion for educating on signs of Spirit Communication and how to live a healthier, happier and more successful life.
