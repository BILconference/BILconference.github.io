Blake Mycoskie is the founder of TOMS Shoes, which he started in 2006 after seeing children without shoes while traveling in Argentina, and creator of its One for One model: for every pair sold, the company gave a pair to a child in need. By the early 2010s TOMS had given away over two million pairs, and his 2011 book Start Something That Matters was a New York Times bestseller.
