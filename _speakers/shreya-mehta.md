She is charged of being unabashedly filmy, but she is not guilty about it. Shreya Mehta, fondly known as the Filmy Kudi is an actor, a dancer, an anchor and a social media executive par excellence. A bollywood buff at heart, her next venture is 'Namastey England' starring Arjun Kapoor and Parineeti Chopra. She is an actor at the very famous youtube channel - The Timeliners. Her past work includes a web series named 'The Aam Aadmi Family' and working with channels like Girliyapa and The Viral Fever (TVF).
