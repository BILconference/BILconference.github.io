Jhon Arokiasamy is a Chennai-based communications and political strategy consultant who founded Persona Leadership Advisory, incorporated in Chennai in 2015, after earlier stints at the PR firms Good Relations India and Perfect Relations. He went on to build the political consulting practice JPAC-Persona.
