Evan Van Ness is the author of Week in Ethereum News, a widely read weekly newsletter covering the Ethereum protocol and ecosystem that he began publishing in 2016. Around 2017 he worked with ConsenSys, the Ethereum-focused venture studio, and was active as an early Ethereum adopter and investor.
