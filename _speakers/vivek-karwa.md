Vivek Karwa is a Chennai-based Certified Financial Planner and investment adviser who has worked in financial services since 2002 and runs the advisory practice VRIDHI, serving retail investors, HNIs, and NRIs. He is a regular commentator on personal finance and stock markets on English, Hindi, and Tamil television channels.
