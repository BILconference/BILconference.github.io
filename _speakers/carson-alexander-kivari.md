Carson Kivari MA RCC is a psychotherapist and fitness coach who educates the public not only through traditional peer-reviewed research, but through singing songwriting, written and freestyle hip-hop, filmmaking and podcasting. He helps to run Thrive Consulting and the Veterans Transition Network in downtown Vancouver.
