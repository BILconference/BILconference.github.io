Neil Gaiman is an English author of novels, comics, and screenplays, best known for the comic series The Sandman and novels including American Gods, Coraline, and Stardust; his The Graveyard Book won both the Newbery and Carnegie Medals. Around his BIL era he made frequent joint appearances with musician Amanda Palmer, whom he married in 2011.
