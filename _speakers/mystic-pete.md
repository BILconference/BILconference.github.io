Mystic Pete is an author, radio host, cellist, music and video producer, spiritual teacher, aphorist, and father of two. He has taught and organized events internationally connecting spirituality and the arts (music, dance, storytelling, and writing).

He hosts a popular radio show,[“In A Dream,”](http://www.facebook.com/InADreamWithMysticPete) one of the only broadcast shows on the West Coast featuring the DJ community, on [KXLU](http://www.kxlu.com/schedule.htm), 88.9 fm, Los Angeles, Saturday nights at midnight PT.
