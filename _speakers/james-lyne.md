James Lyne is a British cybersecurity researcher who was Global Head of Security Research at Sophos in the mid-2010s and a certified instructor with the SANS Institute. Around 2014 he was known for public research projects such as the "World of Warbiking" tour, riding a sensor-equipped bicycle through major cities to document insecure Wi-Fi networks.
