Johann Gevers is a South African-born entrepreneur who founded and led Monetas, a Zug-based cryptofinance company building digital payment services on distributed ledger technology. After moving Monetas to Zug in 2013 he became a founding figure of the region's "Crypto Valley" blockchain ecosystem and of the Crypto Valley Association, launched in January 2017; later that year he became president of the Tezos Foundation.
