is the president of [Students For Liberty](http://www.studentsforliberty.org), a US-based nonprofit organization whose mission is to educate, develop, and empower the next generation of leaders of liberty. Since SFL's founding in 2008, the organization has grown to include over 2,000 student groups, 1,000 trained student leaders, helping over 90,000 students each year. He is also a member of the Board of Advisors of The Atlas Society and an honorary board member of the Our America Initiative. In his spare time, he likes to read, write, cook, climb, and visit a cabin in the middle of nowhere, Pennsylvania.
