Prateep V. Philip is a 1987-batch Indian Police Service officer of the Tamil Nadu cadre who around 2017 served as Additional Director General of Police heading the state's Economic Offences Wing in Chennai. He founded the Friends of Police community policing initiative in 1993, which received the Queen's Award for Innovation in Police Training and Development in 2002. He survived the 1991 bombing that assassinated Rajiv Gandhi, and retired in 2021 as Director of the Tamil Nadu Police Academy.
