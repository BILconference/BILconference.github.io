Brad Templeton is a software architect and internet pioneer who founded ClariNet Communications, one of the first internet-based businesses, and chaired the Electronic Frontier Foundation's board from 2000 to 2010. Around 2014 he chaired the Networks and Computing track at Singularity University and consulted for Google's self-driving car project, writing extensively on autonomous vehicles.
