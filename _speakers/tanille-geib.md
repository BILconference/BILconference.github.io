Tanille Geib is a Lover of Humans. She is a writer, director, producer, erotic artist, community builder, and passionate cook. She has produced over 300 artists in all art disciplines in the past decade in Calgary and Vancouver. She is an award-winning writer and her book "Love &amp; Cooking, The Erotic Cookbook" was published in April 2013. Currently she is passionately producing a live talk show experience called The Sexuality Salon where community comes together to experience, learn, inspire different aspects of sexuality through performance, panel discussion and media critique. You can check out more of her projects at www.tanillegeib.ca
