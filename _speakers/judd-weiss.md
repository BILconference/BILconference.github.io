Judd Weiss is an entrepreneur in the purest sense of the word. The man has his hands in more things than I can list. He unleashes in a slightly muzzled manner on his blog Hustle Bear. Judd believes that you can transform yourself if you are willing and he has successfully lead real estate brokerages in the extremely competitive market of Los Angeles now he has stepped into areas that include parking. He was a millionaire at 24 and lives what he earns.
His previous talk at BIL has been worth every minute with an extremely good reputation once he was done. He knows how to sell without you knowing you've been sold to.
