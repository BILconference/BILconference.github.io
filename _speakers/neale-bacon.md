I have had a weight problem most of my life but it was in approximately 1998 when I realized I had a reach a turning point. One morning I stepped on the scale and was shocked.

You see we had a heavy duty scale that went to 425 pounds. When I stepped on, I heard "clink". That clink was the needle going all the way around and trying to go farther. I was 425 plus!I had 2 young children at the time. I didn't want to die and leave my wife alone with the 2 kids. I knew I had to do something.

Over the years I tried a lot of fad diets and things that didn't work, but then I learned the secret. Are you ready? Eat less and move more! That of course is the simple answer.

In the course of my journey, the biggest thing I learned besides healthy eating choices and exercise is WHY I was eating the way I was that lead me to 425+ and as of today, I have so far last 185 pounds with no pills, potions, shakes, short cuts or surgery.

Combining my skills as a ventriloquist and performer, I am now bringing a message of real weight loss, and the importance of healthy eating and exercise. I am also a member of the Canadian Obesity Network as well as the National Exercise and Sports Trainers Association.

Let me tell you how bad eating choices and the obesity epidemic is effecting you, your friends and even your business. Let's laugh and learn together!
