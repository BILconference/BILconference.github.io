Project Manager and Photographer based in Vancouver via London, England. I'm an advocate for people getting connected to what's important to them in their life, and how they can achieve it and love learning people's stories and bringing them together. I'm a keen and excitable connector and can often be found at events around Vancouver or organizing them myself. My heart and passion is in social impact and innovation and creating something bigger than one person, and working on projects that will make a positive impact and difference in the wider community and the world. I'm on the board of Cameras4Change, a Vancouver based non-profit who work with marginalized and vulnerable communities by providing them with educational photography workshops and the opportunity to transform through creative self-expression, and a co-founder of Atomik Change, a recently registered non-profit to work with people going through transitional periods in their life.
