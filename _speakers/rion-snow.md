Rion Snow is a PhD Candidate in Computer Science at Stanford University, working with Professors Andrew Ng and Dan Jurafsky. … His work on automatically inferring semantic taxonomies received the Best Paper Award at the 2006 conference of the Association of Computational Linguistics.
