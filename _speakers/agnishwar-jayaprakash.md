Agnishwar Jayaprakash is a Chennai-born former Indian national swimmer who set multiple national records and won six gold medals at the 2007 South Asian Games. After retiring from competitive swimming he founded Ignite-India, a student innovation and entrepreneurship platform, and the Agni Foundation, while serving in leadership roles in his family's Agni Group of educational institutions. He later founded the drone company Garuda Aerospace.
