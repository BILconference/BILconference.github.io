Jordan Matthew Yerman has been around the world almost three times, and has cultivated a love of photography as a storytelling tool. He documents cities through the lived experiences of their inhabitants, be they human or otherwise.

Yerman’s work has been published by the Vancouver Observer, APEX Experience Magazine, Akihabara News, Tokyo INSIGHT Magazine, Love Meow, Vancity Buzz, Vancouver Courier, Gripped, and Canadian Avalanche Journal; as well as being featured on Buzzfeed. He takes high-stakes cat photos for Vancouver Orphan Kitten Rescue, helping stray cats find new homes. He has presented on street cat photography at BIL2015 in Vancouver.
