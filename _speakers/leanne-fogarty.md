Leanne is trained as a BC SPCA Cruelty Investigation Officer and is completing the Investigation & Enforcement Skills Certificate at the Justice Institute of BC. Recently she went undercover and visited 7 elephant facilities in Thailand. She saw first-hand how elephants were mistreated, as well as the facilities that truly respected their welfare. The results will surprise you.
