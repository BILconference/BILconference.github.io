Tee Crane is a teacher, public speaker and author. His areas of expertise are in Emotional Literacy, Relationship Building and Personal Development. He is co-founder of 3Source Enterprises, an international training company that assists people in resetting, redesigning and relaunching their lives in a direction with fulfillment as its purpose.
