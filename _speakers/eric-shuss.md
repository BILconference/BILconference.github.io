Mr. Shuss has been actively involved in running high-tech companies for over thirty years.  He has owned and managed successful companies from start-up to thriving on-going ventures including I.T./ERP sales and consulting firms, high-tech manufacturers, retail operations and distributors.  He has over 25 years of business management experience with a keen understanding of how technology impacts today’s business.   His lifelong passion is bringing new technologies to market in the Robotics and Artificial Intelligence arena.
