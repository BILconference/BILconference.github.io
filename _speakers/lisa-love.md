Lisa Love loves people, dancing and trees. Founder of Tree Our World - working with farmers/gardeners in 7 countries, I am co-creating a Global Advocacy Network for Moringa Tree of Life (the world's most nutritious and sustainable food) and in development for my film Tre*Evolution. My human and earth rights journey began young in 1971 at age 8 fundraising and recycling with SPEC and Greenpeace! If you are inspired to Tree Our World or participate in Global Sustainability, please check out our page https://www.facebook.com/treeourworldnow and the Vancouver TOW Group https://www.facebook.com/groups/treeourworld/
