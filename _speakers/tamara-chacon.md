Tamara earned her BA in Communication Studies from Loyola Marymount University, where she co-founded an organization called Thinking With that uses creative, empathy-based problem solving methods to approach social justice issues on both an individual and a systemic level. Her passion for social justice has led her to do work on multiple altruistic ventures, and she volunteers for organizations that work with the homeless and fight environmental injustice in her spare time.
