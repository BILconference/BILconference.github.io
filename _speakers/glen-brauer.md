Glen Brauer is passionate about connecting people in meaningful ways. Although trained as a software developer, Glen has spent the last five years coordinating meaningful discussions in over 25 cities with The Meaningful Discussions Project. Glen is also the founder and coordinator of Calopia; the cause shopping network.
