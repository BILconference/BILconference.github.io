I'm the Co-Founder of MetroQuest.com. My focus for the past 20+ years has been on providing leadership in the development and use of digital tools for broad and meaningful stakeholder engagement and public outreach. Most of my efforts have been on enhancing community participation for transportation, urban and sustainability planning projects. My biggest satisfaction is witnessing the positive impact that public input can have on a plan. I believe we need smart plans that have the public and political support to be carried through to successful implementation.
