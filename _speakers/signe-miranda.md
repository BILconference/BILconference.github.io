Signe is a professional songwriter and musician, performing her original songs and recording for over a decade; writing songs for over 20 years. She released three solo and band albums, toured across Canada and in the UK & Norway, leads songwriting workshops, and is author of the #1 international best selling book, """"The Song Creation Formula: 7 Easy Steps to Writing Your Song."
