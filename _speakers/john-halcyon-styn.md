Halcyon is a 13 year Burning Man participant, 2-time Webby Award winner, and former host of NBC.com's "Fears. Regrets. Desires."

He is also co-founder of 1stSaturdays (facebook.com/helpthehomeless), a San Diego Homeless outreach program that leans heavily on Burning Man principals
