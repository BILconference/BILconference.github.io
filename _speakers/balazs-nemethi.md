Balázs Némethi is a Hungarian entrepreneur who founded Taqanu, a blockchain-based banking startup intended to give refugees and people without fixed addresses access to financial services by building a digital identity from their digital footprint. In 2017 Taqanu presented its financial-inclusion concept to central bankers and policymakers at a forum during the G20 summit in Hamburg.
