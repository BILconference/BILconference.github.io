Sagarikka Sivakumar is a Chennai-based author who, at 15, took a year off after her tenth standard and wrote My Unskooled Year (2016) about the experience. During that year she invested in stocks, interned, trekked, blogged, and made documentaries, and went on to speak publicly about rethinking exam-focused education.
