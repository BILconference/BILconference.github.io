Will is the Vice President for Special Projects at Virgin Galactic, the world's first spaceline. Virgin Galactic, part of Sir Richard Branson's Virgin Group, is building on the legacy of the award-winning SpaceShipOne effort by bringing a new fleet of commercial spacecraft for private, civil, and scientific passengers into service.
