Charlie Glickman PhD is the Education Program Manager at Good Vibrations, a teacher, blogger, writer, and occasional university professor. His areas of expertise include sex-positivity, sexual shame, communities of erotic affiliation, sexual practices and relationship structures, and adult sexuality education.
