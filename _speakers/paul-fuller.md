Paul Fuller is an entrepreneur, strategic adviser, and “Astropreneur” specializing in Aerospace startup accounting for clients including startups such as Space Frontier Foundation and Cosmic Lifestyle Corporation. Paul also provides works with established organizations and non-aerospace startups including the City of Salinas and Vintage Electric Bikes. An energetic problem solver of financial systems and startup strategies, he advises start-ups and not-for-profits on innovation and business strategy. He has held accounting roles with Clinica de Salud and the Department of Defense, and founded a startup accounting and consultancy to maintain involvement in the startup community. He has served as a Student Representative for Ethics In America Conference at West Point and serves as Treasurer of the Space Frontier Foundation and as a living history presenter for Victorian and Civil War education. Learn more at CogworksConsulting.com.
