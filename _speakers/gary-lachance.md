Hi there. My name is Gary and I'm the co-founder of "Tom and Gary's Decentralized Dance Party" AKA "The DDP" AKA "The Party Revolution." For the past four years, we've been relentlessly touring North America, organizing revolutionary acts of shared experience on a scale made possible only by cutting-edge technology. Our Parties are free, open to anyone, organized through social media, powered by crowdfunding and hosted exclusively in public spaces. The DDP takes the limitless connectivity of an online community and translates it back into the physical world, creating an ever-expanding feedback loop of social collaboration and individual empowerment.
