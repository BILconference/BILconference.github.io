Mr. Muthiah Ramanathan, Director – Training of Mind Dynamics Center, has served as the President of the Hypnotique Circle – Chennai for the past 8 years.

Serves as Facilitator in Bodhi Organizational and People Consultants Pvt. Ltd., Chennai.
Serves as Faculty in the Academy of Insurance Marketing and Management. Chennai.

He is a Certified Trainer in N L P from the National Federation of Neuro Linguistic Psychology (School of Mr. William Horton) Florida, U S A.
He is also a certified Practitioner of N L P from the School of Mr. Dick McHugh, U S A.

Has undergone extensive Training in Advanced Transactional Analysis from the I T A A – International Transactional Analysis Association, U S A.

Has undergone Trainers’ Training from ‘Indian Society for Training and Development, Chennai, Oscar Murphy International, Bangalore and Editin’, Hyderabad

Has done his Post-graduation in Psychology from the University of Madras and Psychotherapy from the Institute of Psychotherapy and Management Sciences, Mumbai.

Has done his Post Graduate Diploma in Guidance and Counseling from the Annamalai University and Post Graduate Diploma in Psychotherapy and Counseling from the Institute of Psychotherapy and Management Sciences, Mumbai.

Has presented Training programs for more than 20 years on Management, H R D, Organizational Behaviour, Psychology and Personality Development to well over 25,000 people all over India. Has also presented programs in Malaysia and Sri Lanka.

Has presented Papers on ‘Psychoanalysis, Transactional Analysis and Hypno-analysis’ in the International Conference of Indian Academy of Applied Psychology, National Conference of Transactional Analysis and International Conference on Sexology.

Has given Interviews and presented programs in Doordarshan, Jaya TV, Makkal TV, Vasanth TV, Tamil Radio Television, Tamil Television Network, Malaysian Radio and All India Radio.

Has created Audio Cassettes on Physical and Psychological wellbeing on the topics ‘The Art of Relaxation, Stress Management, Time Management’ etc.

Has written 2 Books in Tamil on ‘The Powers of the Sub-conscious Mind and Hypnosis’ and ‘How to score more than 90%’ for Plus II Students published by Kannadhasan Pathippagam, Chennai.

He specializes in Behavioural Psychology and Hypnosis.
