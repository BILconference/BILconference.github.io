Yashasvini Rajeshwar is a great example of the bubbling energy that characterizes urban Indian youth today. After her first few years of schooling in North America, she quickly adapted to the Indian system, first in a mainstream school followed by a longer stint in a less common learning environment. She excelled in both the Cambridge IGCSE as well as A level exams, and is currently preparing to begin her undergraduate studies in the liberal arts.

Yashasvini has several diverse interests that keep her busy…
