Nigel Mojica is an event host, program facilitator, and hip-hop artist based in Vancouver, BC. For over a decade, Nigel has worked with at-risk segments of the population on initiatives promoting wellbeing, community engagement, economic participation and health. He is a lover of stories, a student of psychology, and a strong believer in meeting people where they are.
