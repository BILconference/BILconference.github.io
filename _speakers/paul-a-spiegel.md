Paul A. Spiegel is a Transhumanist lawyer active in the fields of technology, biotechnology, intellectual property, trademarks, copyright and the arts. He is active in longevity issues, sitting on the boards of the International Longevity Alliance and the American Longevity Alliance. In his spare time, he is working on a constitution for an immortal humanity, promoting a legal structure delineating the rights and responsibilities of minds uploaded to non-biological substrates. Since 2009, he has followed a course of Ayahuasca shamanism, including the use of plant medicines for physical and spiritual healing.
