Caroline is a philosopher, entrepreneur and outdoor enthusiast living in the mountains of Whistler, BC.

She is the founder of Impact Economy, and co-founder of The Citizens Media - a communications platform for connecting communities, needs and solutions as we transition to a new way of working, where people and planet can thrive.

With a background in network management, Caroline is a key convener of conversations, that are of interest and importance in new economy thinking. She is driven by what she sees as an 'infinite realm of possibilities', which become apparent when people, communities, and values unite.

An innovative and highly respected thinker, Caroline is passionate about building systems – based on real life circumstance – to bring about collaboration, that help society to prosper, and humanity to grow.
