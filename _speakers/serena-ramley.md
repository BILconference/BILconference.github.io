"Serena leads UBC Sailbot, an engineering team at the University of British Columbia that designs and builds fully autonomous sailboats. After surpassing the U.S. Naval Academy team with the farthest Atlantic sailbot voyage in 2016, the team is now working on a next-generation vessel for the Vic-Maui Yacht Race."
