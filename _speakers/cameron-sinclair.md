Cameron Sinclair is co-founder of Architecture for Humanity, the nonprofit he started in 1999 with Kate Stohr to provide pro-bono design and construction services to communities in need. By 2012 the organization had worked in dozens of countries on schools, clinics, housing, and post-disaster reconstruction, and that year he co-published the second Design Like You Give a Damn volume. He won the TED Prize in 2006.
