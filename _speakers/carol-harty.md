Carol Harty has a PhD in clinical biochemistry and a bottomless sense of wonder. A lifelong science geek, she is here to spread the word about a bold new set of tools to get people off their glossy iDevices and into creative action. She will give an overview of how small cheap computers such as Raspberry Pi and Arduino are transforming DIY electronics, and the worlds of possibilities that are opening up as more and more people become Makers.
