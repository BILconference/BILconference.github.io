Karim Benabdallah is a Tunisian IT and communications specialist who founded BIL Conference Tunisia in 2012 and served as its curator, growing it from an initial audience of about 60 people into a series of more than 25 events. Around 2017 he was also an organizer of Social Good Summit Tunisia and an advisor to the BIL Collaborative, working as a digital marketing coach focused on youth empowerment.
