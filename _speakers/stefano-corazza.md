Stefano Corazza is the founder and CEO of mixamo.com, the first online 3D character animation platform. Former Stanford University researcher, Stefano holds two PhDs in engineering and bioengineering, and a master in design. He is also a 3D/robotic media artists, with interactive art installations featured at The Dallas Museum of Nature and Science, San Francisco Academy of Science, Burning Man, among others.
