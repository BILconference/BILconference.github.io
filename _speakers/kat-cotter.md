Dr. Kat Cotter is a chiropractor and an authority on anti-aging. She has spent the last 25 years researching everything from ancient secrets to the latest cutting edge information on ways to slow, stop and reverse aging and she shares this information through speaking engagements and workshops. Her practice centers on holistic health, wellness programs and disease prevention and she creates customized programs to help people slow down the rate at which they are aging. She is the originator of the Longevity Bootcamp and slso does one-on-one coaching.
