Amanda Palmer is an American singer-songwriter and performance artist, known as one half of the punk-cabaret duo the Dresden Dolls. In 2012 she raised nearly $1.2 million from about 25,000 fans in a Kickstarter campaign for her album Theatre Is Evil, then the largest music crowdfunding project to date — an experience that later became the basis of her 2013 TED talk "The Art of Asking."
