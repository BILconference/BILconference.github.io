Matt Elson is an artist exploring concepts of consciousness, perception and beauty. He has been creating The Infinity Boxes since 2012. Early in his career he helped pioneer the field of computer graphics at Symbolics Inc. which evolved out of the MIT Artificial Intelligence Lab. He was DreamWorks Animations Artistic Liaison when the studio started and was integral in helping Walt Disney Feature Animation make the switch from hand drawn animation to computer graphics. He was trained as a painter at Pratt Institute, received his Masters Degree in Communications from the New York Institute of Technology and is a graduate of the UCLA Anderson Executive Program.
