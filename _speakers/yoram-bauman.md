Yoram Bauman is a PhD environmental economist and “[stand-up economist](http://www.standupeconomist.com/).” He is the founder of the revenue-neutral carbon tax proposal (I-732) that will be on the ballot in Washington State in November 2016. He has been working on environmental tax reform since 1998, when he co-authored the book [*Tax Shift*](http://www.sightline.org/publications/books/tax-shift/tax)*, *which[helped inspire the revenue-neutral carbon tax in British Columbia](http://daily.sightline.org/daily_score/archive/2008/03/10/more-on-bc2019s-carbon-tax-shift). Bauman also co-authored the [*Cartoon Introduction to Climate Change*](http://standupeconomist.com/category/books/) and the two-volume [*Cartoon Introduction to Economics*](http://standupeconomist.com/category/books/).
