Alex Wormuth is the CEO and co-founder of HexCare, a platform that provides patient wearable data to doctors. He is also completing his Computer Science degree at USC and has been working at the NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory for 2 years. While at JPL, Alex develops applications for scientists to search, view, and interact with extremely large data sets. His most recent project involves constructing 3D terrain models from Mars rover images. Aside from technology, Alex loves to read, travel, and play piano, soccer, chess, and go.
