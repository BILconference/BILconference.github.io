A non-formal educator with 12 years of experience, who has learned octopuses can reveal surprising truths. After spending some time learning about octopuses, Keely was offered the chance to participate in octopus research in the South Pacific. She dove in head first, saw some sharks, poked some octopuses and learned some valuable things along the way.
