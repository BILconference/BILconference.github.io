Hers is a voice that radio listeners in Delhi will recognise — ‘Sabki Chahiti RJ Sukriti’ from 98.3 Radio Mirchi. The young and free spirited mind, a raw, organic voice, an engineer by qualification found her love as a Radio Jockey. Youngest in her team, RJ Sukriti resounds as a name synonymous to panache, perseverance, zest and vivacity.
