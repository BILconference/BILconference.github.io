Nina Hartley is an American adult film performer, registered nurse, and sex educator, and the author of Nina Hartley's Guide to Total Sex. A prominent sex-positive feminist, she produced instructional videos, lectured widely on sexuality, and appeared in the 1997 film Boogie Nights.
