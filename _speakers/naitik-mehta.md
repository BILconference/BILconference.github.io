Naitik is a 21-year old entrepreneur & designer in Vancouver, Canada. He is currently the co-founder of NextBillion.org – a community for people with disabilities in tech, that provides mentorship & employment opportunities. He has won 20+ awards, worked with corporates, design agencies, social impact NGOs & founded 2 startups.
