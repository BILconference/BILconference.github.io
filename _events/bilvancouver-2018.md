BIL is proud to be hosting its 5th annual event in beautiful Vancouver, BC (right after that other event a little ways down the road past Gastown). This year we will be hosted at the [Subculture](http://creativecoworkers.com/subculture/) space in Railtown - aka creative Co-workers - a seminal co-working space in Van. We will be returning to our true unconference roots with a crowd assembled at 10am *avec* coffee and organic doughnuts to determine the topics of the day. Open to the public and fully participant and volunteer driven, our yearly gathering features a wild mix of technologists, scientists, artists, hackers, and those with a passion for community awareness, social entrepreneurship, and innovation.
Come one and come all. Once again in the spirit of radical accessibility, admission is by donation (suggested $10). [Register HERE](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/bil-vancouver-2018-tickets-44821683863?aff=efbeventtix).
