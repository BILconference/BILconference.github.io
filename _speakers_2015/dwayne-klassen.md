Dwayne Klassen is an international men and relationship expert. He’s a transformational speaker, author and empowerment coach. He’s known a “The Coach For Men”. Dwayne has a passion for helping humanity raise the bar and play a bigger game. Dwayne’s “Perfect Storm” was what it took to “loose it all”, but also the perfect lessons to help him be the inspiration he is today. He's the author of “The Remarkable Man" and the Founder of The Remarkable Man Project - A global initiative to challenge 1 million men to step up and play a bigger game. The vision for the project is to create the greatest leadership, empowerment and adventure network in the world. A place where men get the resources, tools and brotherhood to be Remarkable for all human kind.
