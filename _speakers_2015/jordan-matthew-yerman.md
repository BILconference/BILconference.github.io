talk_title: 'The Street Cat Project: Exploring Cities through the Eyes of Feral Cats'
talk_description: The Street Cat Project explores the relationship that we as city-dwellers
  have with our nonhuman neighbors. Jordan Matthew Yerman has traveled to Japan, Iceland,
  and Israel to meet their local street-cat populations. Profound differences emerged
  in how these feline street urchins behave around humans, and how they interact with
  one another. This exploration has come full circle, as he encountered Metro Vancouver’s
  own carefully-hidden feral cat population.

Jordan Matthew Yerman is a Vancouver-based writer and photographer. He has been published by the Vancouver Observer, APEX Experience Magazine, Akihabara News, Love Meow, Gripped, and Canadian Avalanche Journal; as well as presenting for the ProductYVR series.
