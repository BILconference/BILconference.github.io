Ricky Shetty is the owner and operator of DaddyBlogger.com. Ricky's parents had a divorce and he didn't have a strong father figure growing up so he has made it his personal mission to help support dads through his blog, his book, and his group YVR Dads! Ricky also runs YVR Bloggers (a community of 1000+ Vancouver bloggers) as well as several conferences a year including the Social Media Mastery Conference, the Blog Mastery Conference, and the Internet Marketing Mastery Conference!
