## Event facts (confirmed)

- **Dates:** Saturday–Sunday, March 5–6, 2011 (the weekend after TED2011; BIL's 4th year).
- **Venue:** The Queen Mary, 1126 Queens Highway, Long Beach, CA 90802 (permanently docked — "this is not a cruise").
- **Scale:** ~600 expected (WSJ, Mar 1 2011). Free and open, unconference format — most talks self-listed on the wiki before the event, final schedule set day-of.
- **Lead organizer:** Simone Syed ("Spearheading BIL 2011"); **MC:** Baron R.K. "Reichart" Von Wolfsheild.
- Sources: bilconference.com blog post 2011-02-13 (Wayback 20110218021955), homepage 20110208, `_events/bil-2011.md` (WP event record), pbworks wiki, WSJ 2011-03-01.

## Primary source discovered

The single best line-up source is the period planning wiki page **"BIL 2011 - Speakers"**
(bilconference.pbworks.com, still live; canonical URL below). It is a pre-event self-signup
roster ("Do you plan to speak at BIL? Add your Talk below the last one.") with full talk
titles, abstracts, and speaker bios, tagged `BIL2011, BIL, Queen Mary, Speakers`, with a
period comment (Jenn Ramsey, 1:58 am Mar 5, 2011) linking the shared speaker calendar
(tinyurl.com/bil2011).

- Speakers page: http://bilconference.pbworks.com/w/page/36738653/BIL%202011%20-%20Speakers
  (saved: `wayback2011/pbworks-bil2011-speakers.html|.txt` — full verbatim abstracts and bios there)
- Organizers page: http://bilconference.pbworks.com/w/page/36599131/BIL%202011%20-%20Organizers
  (saved: `wayback2011/pbworks-bil2011-organizers.html|.txt`)

**Caveat for all wiki rows:** the page is the announced/self-listed roster. Being an
unconference, delivery of each individual talk is not independently confirmed unless noted.

### bilconference.com — "BIL 2011" (posted Feb 13, 2011, Upcoming Conferences)
Source: http://bilconference.com/upcoming-conferences/bil-2011/ via
https://web.archive.org/web/20110218021955id_/http://bilconference.com/upcoming-conferences/bil-2011/

> BIL is an unconference for people changing the world in big ways. It's a place for passionate people to come together to energize, brainstorm, and take action. BIL returns the weekend after TED2011 for the fourth year in a row. Join other innovative thinkers, including many TED attendees, on Saturday and Sunday, March 5th and 6th on the Queen Mary in Long Beach, CA to curate the collaboration that turns big ideas into reality.
>
> BIL is organized by a network of individuals from around the country who are devoted to expanding knowledge and exploring the unconventional. BIL has always been and will remain free to the public. Our goal is to curate a medium for you to engage in others' passions and find inspiration from great human accomplishments.
>
> Rather than recruit presenters, BIL uniquely invites speakers to step forward the day of the show. To experience this ballistic, intelligent, and legendary event, register (here) and ensure you are a part of the unconference that is BIL: Inspired by TED, created by you.
>
> NOTE: The Queen Mary is permanently docked, this is not a cruise.

### Wall Street Journal — Erica Orden, "For BIL, Tagging Along With TED Proves to Be an Excellent Adventure" (The A-Hed, March 1, 2011)

> For everyone else, there's BIL. BIL is the "unconference" that has been a tagalong with TED since 2008, held the same week and as close as possible to the more glittering gathering.

> "BIL sort of formed out of a desire to shadow TED," says Eric Gradman, a roboticist who spoke at the first BIL, in 2008.

> Says co-founder Cody Marx Bailey: "The general rule is, have low expectations." "If you're worried about there being a chair, you might want to bring one," he added.

> BIL, which expects 600 people when it opens Saturday, is free, lets anyone attend and doesn't schedule speakers until the morning it opens, depending on who shows up.
