## bil-fo — BIL:Fo (Tunis, June 2013)

Sources checked: stub; Wayback event page (empty); YouTube; searches around what
"Fo" means.

**Zero named speakers, but the event's likely nature was identified.** The
Freedom Online Coalition's 3rd annual conference ("Joint Action for Free
Expression on the Internet") was held in **Tunis, 17–18 June 2013** — exactly the
stub's June 2013 window — and the BIL Tunisia channel holds a 100-minute video
"Freedom Online   BIL Conference" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oeFZ3WfT2Xk
(uploaded 2013-10-02). Reading: BIL:Fo was BIL Tunisia's session held around the
Freedom Online conference. Confidence: medium (the expansion "Fo" = "Freedom
Online" is inferred from the date match + the channel video; no period text
spells it out).

Freedom Online Tunis 2013 context: 489 participants from 51 countries;
side-events included the ATI #404 lab (sources:
https://freedomonlinecoalition.com/annual-conference/tunis/,
https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2013/06/tunis-coalition). Do not import FO
Coalition plenary speakers as BIL:Fo speakers — no roster of the BIL session
itself was found.

Future lead: the 100-minute video may contain introductions naming speakers.

## bil-fst — BIL:FST / BILFST 2013 (Faculté des Sciences de Tunis; stub March 2013, photos posted 4 March 2013)

Sources checked: stub; Wayback event pages (both generations, empty); Tumblr (30
photos tagged #BILFST posted 2013-03-04, no captions); Google Group threads;
nizarus blog; CLLFST site (cllfst.org has no usable archive); press searches.

**Zero named speakers.** Solid organizational context:

- Organizer: **CLLFST — Club des Logiciels Libres de la Faculté des Sciences de
  Tunis** (the FST free-software club, founded March 2004).
- Theme: **« Set minds free »** — per Nizar Kerkeni's blog retrospective of the
  club's year ("l'année 2013 a été marquée par l'organisation du BILFST sous le
  thème « Set minds free »", blog.nizarus.tn, tag CLLFST — surfaced via search;
  live host unreachable, page not individually archived).
- Initiation (verbatim gist from Google Group thread "Demande BIL a la FST",
  AbdelKarim BenAbdallah forwarding Meriam Bouguerra, 2013-01-21,
  https://groups.google.com/g/biltn/c/rU3-r4CH_e8): Meriam Bouguerra, engineering
  student in Computer Science at FST and member of CLL-FST, requested to host a
  BIL at the faculty; proposed themes included "entrepreneurs and open source"
  and "Start from Scratch (success stories)"; she wrote she had "several
  potential speakers in mind". (Organizer-side figure — NOT a speaker.)
- Photographic evidence the event happened: ~30 #BILFST photos posted to the BIL
  Tunisia Tumblr on 2013-03-04 (e.g.
  https://biltunisia.tumblr.com/post/44546850534/bilfst-bilcomference-biltunisia-biltn)
  — implying the event was on/just before 4 March 2013 (stub's March window).
